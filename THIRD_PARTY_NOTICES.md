# Provenance and third-party components

The modified research workbench derives from selected files in
[SitianGao/geoscope](https://github.com/SitianGao/geoscope), competition commit
`df802ab54b8f4ee496ca83c104bfaee44f7ee888`. The original competition repository
is preserved. Selected modifications are MIT licensed in this research distribution.
Git history and `docs/origin.json` retain the code's source provenance. Repository
account attribution is separate from a confirmed manuscript author list.

The workbench MIT license does not grant rights to any separately installed
dependency, model service, checkpoint, dataset or user-uploaded image. Review
the installed versions and their upstream terms for your own deployment.

The original files under `model_services/` implement HTTP adaptation and process
supervision. They contain no RemoteAgent/RemoteSAM architecture or checkpoints.
RemoteSAM architecture is obtained separately at a fixed upstream revision; its
upstream repository does not supply a root license at that revision. The adapters'
MIT license does not change upstream rights. Exact source/model identities and
the boundary between competition modifications and the independent adapters are
recorded in [model source provenance](docs/model_source_provenance.md).

## Assets and evaluation data

Procedural examples are generated by `fixtures.py`. The additional real-image
example in `examples/data/san-francisco-bay` derives from NASA photograph
ISS004-E-10288. Its source, credit, derivative processing and usage terms are
recorded there; NASA has not endorsed this software. MIT does not relicense the
NASA photograph. Its bundled color-baseline mask is generated by project code. Original competition demonstration images, private model settings,
third-party source trees and weights are excluded. Arial/Helvetica are named as
font choices; proprietary font files are not distributed.

The earlier application study used LoveDA and iSAID/DOTA images and existing
dataset human annotations. Raw images/labels remain outside this source release;
download them from their authoritative providers subject to their dataset terms.
Numerical results are retained with their frozen protocol and source identity.
MIT licensing of the workbench does not relicense those datasets or annotations.

## NAIP example and optional signing

The Denver chip is USDA NAIP distributed by USGS The National Map, catalog tile
`m_3910417_nw_13_060_20190803` (2019). Its public-domain provenance, service export,
coordinate transformation and separate imagery terms are recorded under
`examples/data/naip-denver/`. Credit USDA-FSA-APFO / USGS The National Map.

The optional signing extra uses Cryptography's Ed25519 implementation under its
upstream Apache-2.0/BSD terms. GeoMaskLab includes no upstream cryptography source
or bundled private signing keys. Key ownership and trust remain external decisions.
